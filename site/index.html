Open source

Your Android phone and your computer, as one.

Seam makes your Android phone and your computer work as one, like Google's phone-and-laptop features, but for any Android phone and any Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Open source.

Download See features

How it works

  1. 1

    Install on your computer

    Download Seam for Mac, Windows or Linux and open it. It shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Install on your phone

    Download the Android app and open the .apk. Allow installing from your browser.

  3. 3

    Scan the QR code

    Scan the code with the phone app. No cable, no USB debugging. You are paired.

Features

Notifications

Phone notifications on your computer, and dismiss them from there.

Battery

The battery of your phone, shown on the computer.

Mirror and control

Mirror and control your phone in a window on the computer. Built on scrcpy, nothing extra to install.

Clipboard

Clipboard shared between phone and computer. Coming.

Private pairing

No cable, no USB debugging. Pair by scanning a QR code. Everything goes over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted. No cloud, no account.

Updates itself

Seam updates itself on the computer and on the phone.

Your data never leaves your network

Pair by scanning a QR code with the phone app. Everything goes over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted (TLS with the certificate pinned through the QR code). No cloud, no account.
