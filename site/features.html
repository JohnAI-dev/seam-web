Features

Seam makes your Android phone and your computer work as one, for any Android phone and any Mac, Windows or Linux computer. Open source.

Phone notifications on your computer

Notifications from your phone show up on your computer, and you can dismiss them from there.

Battery

The battery of your phone is shown on the computer.

Mirror and control

Mirror and control your phone in a window on the computer. Built on scrcpy, nothing extra to install.

Clipboard

A clipboard shared between phone and computer is coming.

Pairing

No cable, no USB debugging: pair by scanning a QR code with the phone app. Everything goes over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted (TLS with the certificate pinned through the QR code). No cloud, no account.

Updates itself

Seam updates itself on the computer and the phone.

Download

Seam is open source. Your data stays on your network.
